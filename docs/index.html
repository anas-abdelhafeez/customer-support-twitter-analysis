What this means

  • The median hides the tail: half of customers get an answer in about 24 minutes, but 1 in 6 waits 4+ hours. Track p90, not only the median.
  • 6.7% get silence: 52,139 conversations never got a brand reply, in public.
Where the pressure sits: inbound demand climbs through the day and peaks around 17:00 UTC; the quietest hour is 06:00 UTC, about 2.4x lower.

Conversations by hour and day of week (UTC)

What this means

  • One long evening peak: the six busiest hours of the day are 15:00 to 20:59 UTC, with 269,026 conversations (34% of the total).
  • Weekends are lighter, not empty: Sat and Sun carry 94,180 and 93,573 conversations vs 125,852 on Fri. Social support is a 7-day operation.
Who answers well, and who leaves customers waiting: among the 20 busiest brands, the median wait runs from 2.6 min (TMobileHelp) to 757 min (hulu_support), a gap of about 291x.

What this means

  • Speed at scale exists: AmazonHelp answers 76,473 conversations at a 10.7-minute median; AppleSupport has similar volume at 88.3 minutes.
  • Volume does not explain the gap. Brands of every size sit at both ends of the chart.
Why some customers wait: the median wait barely moves through the day. The tail does. Waits of the slowest 10% jump overnight and again in the evening.

What this means

  • The worst tails: conversations started at 02:00, 01:00, 03:00 UTC have a p90 wait of 729 to 740 minutes.
  • Limit of this data: the dataset has no staffing or ticket-queue fields, so this shows when waits are long, not why. Coverage gaps are a hypothesis to test, not a finding.
When it happens: Fri is the busiest weekday and Sun the quietest in this window. The share answered within an hour is highest late at night and lowest around 04:00 UTC.

Recommendations that this data supports.

Track p90, not only the median

The median (24.3 min) looks healthy. The p90 is 546 minutes. Put the public SLA on p90 and review the over-4h tail weekly.

17.4% wait over 4 hours

Never leave a public thread unanswered

6.7% of conversations got no brand reply. A short holding reply with an ETA beats silence.

52,139 unanswered

Look at the overnight and evening tail

p90 waits are longest for conversations started overnight and around 20:00 UTC. Check coverage for those hours first.
